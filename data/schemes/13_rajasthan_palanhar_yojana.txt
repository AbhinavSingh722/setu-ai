SCHEME NAME: Rajasthan Palanhar Yojana
SCHEME NAME (HINDI): राजस्थान पालनहार योजना
SOURCE URL: https://sje.rajasthan.gov.in
OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://sje.rajasthan.gov.in
CATEGORY: Child Welfare / Social Security
LEVEL: State Government (Rajasthan)
IMPLEMENTING DEPARTMENT: Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Rajasthan

OBJECTIVE:
To provide financial assistance for the upbringing and education of orphan children and other vulnerable categories, ensuring they grow up in a family environment rather than an institution.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Child and caretaker (Palanhar) must be residents of Rajasthan for at least 3 years
- Annual income of caretaker's family must NOT exceed ₹1,20,000 (₹1.2 lakh)
- Children aged 0-6 years must be enrolled in an Anganwadi
- Children aged 6-18 years must be enrolled in a school
- Eligible categories: orphans; children of parents sentenced to death/life imprisonment; children of widowed mothers (up to 3 children); and other vulnerable groups as defined by department
- Caretaker must have Aadhaar-linked bank account for DBT

BENEFITS:
- Children aged 0-6 years: ₹1,500 per month
- Children aged 6-18 years: ₹2,500 per month
- Additional Annual Grant: ₹2,000 per year (for books, stationery, clothes, shoes, etc.)
- Payments via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)

MANDATORY ANNUAL REQUIREMENT:
- Must submit school/Anganwadi continuation certificate annually (typically in July) for uninterrupted payments

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Aadhaar Card of both child and caretaker
- Death certificate of parents (or relevant proof for other categories)
- Income Certificate (annual family income ≤ ₹1,20,000)
- Proof of residence (3+ years in Rajasthan)
- School/Anganwadi enrollment certificate
- Bank Account Details (Aadhaar-linked)
- Passport-size photographs

APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. Apply online via sje.rajasthan.gov.in OR
2. Visit nearest e-Mitra kiosk for application assistance
3. Submit all required documents
4. Application verified by district-level SJE Department
5. Monthly payments begin upon verification

For status checks: Visit Jan Soochna Portal or contact district SJE office
