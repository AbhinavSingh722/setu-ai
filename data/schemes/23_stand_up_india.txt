SCHEME NAME: Stand Up India Scheme
SCHEME NAME (HINDI): स्टैंड अप इंडिया योजना
SOURCE URL: https://standupmitra.in
OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://standupmitra.in
CATEGORY: Entrepreneurship / Finance
LEVEL: Central Government (Central Sector Scheme)
IMPLEMENTING MINISTRY: Ministry of Finance (Department of Financial Services)

OBJECTIVE:
To promote entrepreneurship among SC, ST and women entrepreneurs by providing bank loans between Rs 10 lakh and Rs 1 crore for setting up greenfield enterprises in manufacturing, services, trading or agriculture allied activities.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Must be SC/ST and/or Women entrepreneur (18+ years)
- Loan only for greenfield (new) enterprise, not existing businesses
- For non-individual enterprises: at least 51% shareholding by SC/ST/Women promoter
- Borrower should not be in default to any bank or financial institution
- Each bank branch must extend at least one loan to SC/ST borrower and one to woman borrower
- Enterprise can be in manufacturing, services, trading sector, or agri-allied activities

BENEFITS:
- Bank loan between Rs 10 lakh and Rs 1 crore
- Composite loan covering term loan and working capital both
- Repayment period: up to 7 years with moratorium period of up to 18 months
- Margin money: up to 25% (can be provided from convergence with other schemes)
- Credit Guarantee Cover through Credit Guarantee Fund Scheme for Stand Up India (CGFSI)
- Handholding support during and after loan through Stand Up Connect Centers

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Aadhaar Card and PAN Card
- Caste Certificate (for SC/ST category)
- Identity proof (Voter ID/Passport/Driving License)
- Address proof
- Business Plan/Project Report
- Income Tax Returns (ITR) of last 2 years (if applicable)
- Proof of business premises (rent agreement/ownership documents)
- Passport-size photographs

APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. Visit https://standupmitra.in portal
2. Register as a new user and fill the online application
3. Or visit any scheduled commercial bank branch directly
4. Submit application with project report and required documents
5. Bank evaluates the proposal and business plan
6. If approved, loan is sanctioned and disbursed
7. SIDBI provides handholding support through Stand Up Connect Centers
8. Helpline: 1800-180-1111 (Udyami Helpline)
