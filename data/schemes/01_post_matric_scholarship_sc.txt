SCHEME NAME: Post-Matric Scholarship for Scheduled Caste (SC) Students
SCHEME NAME (HINDI): अनुसूचित जाति के छात्रों के लिए पोस्ट मैट्रिक छात्रवृत्ति
SOURCE URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/pmssc
OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://scholarships.gov.in
CATEGORY: Education / Scholarship
LEVEL: Central Government (Centrally Sponsored Scheme)
IMPLEMENTING MINISTRY: Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment

OBJECTIVE:
To provide financial assistance to Scheduled Caste students studying at post-matriculation or post-secondary level to enable them to complete their education. The scheme aims to prevent dropout due to financial constraints.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Must belong to Scheduled Caste (SC) category
- Must be enrolled in a recognized post-matriculation or post-secondary course (Class 11, 12, undergraduate, postgraduate, PhD, etc.)
- Annual income of parents/guardians from all sources must NOT exceed ₹2,50,000 per annum
- Must be a full-time student in a recognized institution within India
- Students are selected by the State Government/UT where they are permanently domiciled
- Must have passed previous qualifying examination

BENEFITS:
- Maintenance Allowance: Monthly financial aid for hostellers and day scholars (amounts vary based on course group)
- Fee Reimbursement: Reimbursement of compulsory non-refundable fees
- Book Bank: Facilities for specified courses
- Study Tour Charges: For specified courses
- Thesis Typing/Printing: For research scholars
- Additional support for students with disabilities
- All payments via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Valid Caste Certificate (SC)
- Income Certificate (annual family income ≤ ₹2,50,000)
- Mark sheets from previous qualifying examination
- Proof of enrollment in recognized institution
- Aadhaar Card
- Bank account details (Aadhaar-linked)
- Passport-size photographs
- Domicile certificate

APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. Visit the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) at scholarships.gov.in
2. Complete One-Time Registration (OTR) if new user
3. Fill in the scholarship application form
4. Upload required documents
5. Submit for verification by educational institution
6. Application forwarded to State Nodal Agency
