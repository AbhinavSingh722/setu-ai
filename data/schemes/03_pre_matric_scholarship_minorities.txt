SCHEME NAME: Pre-Matric Scholarship Scheme for Minorities
SCHEME NAME (HINDI): अल्पसंख्यकों के लिए प्री-मैट्रिक छात्रवृत्ति योजना
SOURCE URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/pre-matric-scholarship-minorities
OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://scholarships.gov.in
CATEGORY: Education / Scholarship
LEVEL: Central Government
IMPLEMENTING MINISTRY: Ministry of Minority Affairs

OBJECTIVE:
To support students from notified minority communities in their formative educational years (Class 1 to Class 10) and to prevent school dropouts due to financial constraints.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Must belong to one of the notified minority communities: Muslims, Christians, Sikhs, Buddhists, Jains, or Parsis (Zoroastrians)
- Must be studying in Class 1 to Class 10 in a government or private school in India
- Must have secured at least 50% marks in the previous final examination (not applicable for Class 1 admission)
- Annual family income must NOT exceed ₹1,00,000 (₹1 Lakh)
- 30% of scholarships reserved for girl students

BENEFITS:
- Admission/Tuition Fee: Coverage of admission and tuition fees subject to government ceilings
- Maintenance Allowance: Monthly allowance provided (higher amounts for hostellers)
- Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into beneficiary's bank account

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Self-declaration of belonging to minority community
- Mark sheets from previous qualifying examination (showing 50%+ marks)
- Income Certificate (annual family income ≤ ₹1,00,000)
- Aadhaar Card
- Bank account details
- School enrollment proof
- Passport-size photographs

APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. Visit the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) at scholarships.gov.in
2. Complete One-Time Registration (OTR)
3. Fill out the Pre-Matric Scholarship for Minorities application form
4. Upload required documents
5. Submit for verification by school/institute
6. Scholarship disbursed directly via DBT
