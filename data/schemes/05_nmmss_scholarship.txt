SCHEME NAME: National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship Scheme (NMMSS)
SCHEME NAME (HINDI): राष्ट्रीय साधन-सह-मेधा छात्रवृत्ति योजना (NMMSS)
SOURCE URL: https://scholarships.gov.in
OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://scholarships.gov.in
CATEGORY: Education / Scholarship
LEVEL: Central Government (Centrally Sponsored Scheme)
IMPLEMENTING MINISTRY: Ministry of Education, Department of School Education and Literacy

OBJECTIVE:
To provide financial assistance to meritorious students from economically weaker sections to prevent them from dropping out after Class 8 and encourage continuation of education at secondary and higher secondary levels (Class 9 to 12).

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Must be studying in Class 8 at time of appearing for selection examination
- Must have secured at least 55% marks (or equivalent grade) in Class 7 annual examination
- Relaxation of 5% marks for SC/ST and disabled candidates
- Must be enrolled in Government, Government-aided, or local body school
- Students in KVS, JNV, Sainik Schools, and private/unaided residential schools are NOT eligible
- Annual parental income from all sources must NOT exceed ₹3,50,000

SELECTION PROCESS:
- Students must qualify state-level selection examination with two parts:
  - Mental Ability Test (MAT)
  - Scholastic Aptitude Test (SAT)
- Must pass both tests with at least 40% marks in aggregate (32% for SC/ST students)

BENEFITS:
- Scholarship Amount: ₹12,000 per annum (₹1,000 per month)
- Duration: Maximum 4 years, covering Class 9 to Class 12
- Disbursed via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into student's bank account (public sector bank preferred)

RENEWAL CRITERIA (Class 10 to 12):
- Must secure clear promotion from Class 9 to 10 and Class 11 to 12 in first attempt
- Minimum 60% marks in Class 10 (55% for SC/ST students) for scholarship renewal
- Must maintain continuous enrollment in recognized government/government-aided school

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Class 7 mark sheet (showing 55%+ marks)
- Income Certificate (annual parental income ≤ ₹3,50,000)
- School enrollment certificate
- Aadhaar Card
- Bank account details
- Category certificate (if applicable for SC/ST relaxation)

APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. Appear for state-level NMMSS selection examination conducted by State/UT Education Department
2. Applications processed through National Scholarship Portal (NSP) at scholarships.gov.in
3. Check state-specific guidelines and deadlines from State/UT Education Department
