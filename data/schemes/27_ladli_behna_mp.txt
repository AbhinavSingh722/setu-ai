SCHEME NAME: Ladli Behna Yojana (Madhya Pradesh)
SCHEME NAME (HINDI): लाड़ली बहना योजना (मध्य प्रदेश)
SOURCE URL: https://ladlibahna.mp.gov.in
OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://ladlibahna.mp.gov.in
CATEGORY: Women Welfare / Direct Benefit Transfer
LEVEL: State Government (Madhya Pradesh)
IMPLEMENTING DEPARTMENT: Department of Women and Child Development, Government of Madhya Pradesh

OBJECTIVE:
To promote women's economic empowerment and financial independence by providing a monthly financial assistance of Rs 1,250 to eligible women in Madhya Pradesh, ensuring regular income support for health, nutrition, and self-reliance.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Must be a woman resident of Madhya Pradesh
- Married women (including widows, divorced, deserted, and separated women) are eligible
- Age between 21 and 60 years
- Annual family income should be less than Rs 2.5 lakh
- Should not own more than 5 acres of agricultural land
- Should not be an income taxpayer
- Should not be receiving pension of Rs 1,000 or more from government
- Women from all categories (SC/ST/OBC/General) are eligible

BENEFITS:
- Rs 1,250 per month transferred directly to bank account (increased from initial Rs 1,000)
- Rs 15,000 per year total annual benefit
- Payment on the 10th of every month
- Amount transferred via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)
- No conditions or work requirements — unconditional cash transfer
- Coverage for over 1.25 crore women in Madhya Pradesh

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Aadhaar Card (mandatory)
- Samagra ID (Madhya Pradesh family/member ID — mandatory)
- Bank Account passbook (linked with Aadhaar and DBT-enabled)
- Mobile Number
- Passport-size photograph
- Samagra ID must have e-KYC completed

APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. Visit nearest Gram Panchayat office, Ward office, or Camp organized by the government
2. Fill the Ladli Behna application form
3. Provide Aadhaar number, Samagra ID, and bank details
4. Complete e-KYC verification at the camp
5. Photograph and biometric verification done on the spot
6. After verification by Gram Panchayat/Ward, application forwarded to Janpad/Nagar level
7. Final approval at district level
8. Amount credited to bank account from the following month
9. Check status at https://ladlibahna.mp.gov.in
10. Helpline: 0755-2700800 or CM Helpline 181
