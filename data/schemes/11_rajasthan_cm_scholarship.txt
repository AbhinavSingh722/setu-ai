SCHEME NAME: Mukhyamantri Ucch Shiksha Scholarship Yojana (Chief Minister Higher Education Scholarship)
SCHEME NAME (HINDI): मुख्यमंत्री उच्च शिक्षा छात्रवृत्ति योजना
SOURCE URL: https://sje.rajasthan.gov.in
OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://sso.rajasthan.gov.in
CATEGORY: Education / Scholarship
LEVEL: State Government (Rajasthan)
IMPLEMENTING DEPARTMENT: Department of College Education, Government of Rajasthan

OBJECTIVE:
To provide financial assistance to meritorious students from economically weaker sections to help them pursue undergraduate, postgraduate, or research (PhD) studies in Rajasthan.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Must be a permanent resident of Rajasthan
- Must have passed Class 12 examination from Board of Secondary Education, Rajasthan (BSER), Ajmer
- Must have scored at least 60% marks in Class 12
- Must have secured a position within top 1 lakh in board's merit list
- Annual family income must NOT exceed ₹2,50,000
- Must be a regular student in government or recognized non-government higher/technical educational institution within Rajasthan
- Must NOT be receiving any other government scholarship or financial assistance
- Must have valid Jan Aadhaar card, Aadhaar card, and bank account in nationalized bank

BENEFITS:
- Standard Scholarship: ₹500 per month (maximum 10 months) = ₹5,000 per year
- Divyang (Differently-Abled) Students: ₹1,000 per month (maximum 10 months) = ₹10,000 per year (for disability of 40% or more)
- Duration: Maximum 5 years of continuous regular higher education

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Jan Aadhaar Card (mandatory for online application)
- Aadhaar Card
- Class 12 Mark Sheet (showing 60%+ marks and merit position)
- Income Certificate (annual family income ≤ ₹2,50,000)
- Domicile Certificate
- College enrollment proof / Bonafide Certificate
- Bank Account Details (nationalized bank)
- Disability Certificate (if applicable, showing 40%+ disability)

APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. Visit Rajasthan SSO portal at sso.rajasthan.gov.in
2. Log in with SSO credentials or register as new user
3. Navigate to "Scholarship" section under Department of College Education
4. Fill out application form
5. Upload required documents (income certificate, disability certificate if applicable)
6. Submit application
7. Application verified by educational institution and forwarded to nodal officer
