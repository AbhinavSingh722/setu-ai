SCHEME NAME: Post-Matric Scholarship for Other Backward Classes (OBC) Students
SCHEME NAME (HINDI): अन्य पिछड़ा वर्ग के छात्रों के लिए पोस्ट मैट्रिक छात्रवृत्ति
SOURCE URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/pms-obc
OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://scholarships.gov.in
CATEGORY: Education / Scholarship
LEVEL: Central Government (Centrally Sponsored Scheme)
IMPLEMENTING MINISTRY: Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment

OBJECTIVE:
To provide financial assistance to OBC students pursuing post-matriculation or post-secondary studies within India and to reduce dropout rates.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Must belong to Other Backward Classes (OBC) category
- Must be enrolled in a government-recognized post-matriculation or post-secondary course (Class 11, 12, UG, PG, diploma, professional/technical degrees)
- Must be an Indian citizen
- Annual family income must NOT exceed ₹2,50,000
- Minimum 75% attendance in previous academic year typically required
- Must not be receiving scholarship from any other source

BENEFITS:
- Maintenance Allowance: Monthly or annual stipends (varying by course level, hosteller/day scholar)
- Tuition/Academic Fees: Reimbursement of non-refundable tuition and compulsory academic fees
- Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into Aadhaar-seeded bank account

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Valid OBC Certificate (non-creamy layer)
- Income Certificate (annual family income ≤ ₹2,50,000)
- Mark sheets from previous qualifying examination
- Fee receipts
- Aadhaar Card
- Bank account details (Aadhaar-linked)
- Enrollment proof from institution

APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. Visit the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) at scholarships.gov.in
2. Register/Login on the NSP portal
3. Fill in the application form with personal, academic, and family income details
4. Upload required documents (OBC certificate, income certificate, mark sheets, fee receipts)
5. Submit and ensure verification by educational institution
6. Application verified by State Nodal Agency
