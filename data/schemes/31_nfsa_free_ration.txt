SCHEME NAME: National Food Security Act (NFSA) / Free Ration Scheme
SCHEME NAME (HINDI): राष्ट्रीय खाद्य सुरक्षा अधिनियम (एनएफएसए) / मुफ्त राशन योजना
SOURCE URL: https://nfsa.gov.in
OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://nfsa.gov.in
CATEGORY: Food Security / Welfare
LEVEL: Central Government (Central Act)
IMPLEMENTING MINISTRY: Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution

OBJECTIVE:
To provide subsidized/free foodgrains to approximately two-thirds of India's population (about 81.35 crore people) through the Targeted Public Distribution System (TPDS), ensuring food security for the most vulnerable sections of society.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Households identified under state-specific criteria for inclusion in NFSA beneficiary list
- Two categories: Priority Households (PHH) and Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) households
- AAY: Poorest of the poor families including destitute, widows, terminally ill, disabled, 60+ age households, primitive tribal groups, landless labourers, marginal farmers
- PHH: Identified by state governments based on deprivation parameters
- Existing BPL and AAY ration card holders are typically covered
- Pregnant women and lactating mothers get additional entitlement
- Children aged 6 months to 14 years get free meals through Mid-Day Meal and ICDS

BENEFITS:
- AAY households: 35 kg of foodgrains per family per month at:
  - Rice: Rs 3/kg → now FREE (under PM Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana extended to Dec 2028)
  - Wheat: Rs 2/kg → now FREE
  - Coarse grains: Rs 1/kg → now FREE
- PHH: 5 kg of foodgrains per person per month → now FREE
- Pregnant women and lactating mothers: Free meal at Anganwadi + Rs 6,000 maternity benefit
- Children 6-14 years: Free hot cooked meal or take-home ration through Anganwadi/schools
- Under PMGKAY extension: All NFSA beneficiaries get free ration (no charge at all)
- Coverage: 75% rural and 50% urban population

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Ration Card (PHH or AAY category)
- Aadhaar Card (for biometric authentication at Fair Price Shop)
- Family details registered in ration card
- If not on NFSA list: apply for new ration card through state food department portal

APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. Check if you have an existing ration card — visit your state's PDS/food department portal
2. If not covered: Apply for a new ration card at state food department portal or district food office
3. Fill ration card application form (online or offline)
4. Submit with Aadhaar, address proof, income proof, and family details
5. After verification, ration card issued with NFSA category (PHH or AAY)
6. Collect monthly ration from designated Fair Price Shop (FPS)
7. Biometric authentication via Aadhaar at the FPS (Aadhaar-enabled PDS)
8. To check entitlement: SMS or call 1967 (TPDS helpline)
9. Mera Ration App — download for portability and nearest FPS location
10. Helpline: 1800-11-0841 (toll-free)
