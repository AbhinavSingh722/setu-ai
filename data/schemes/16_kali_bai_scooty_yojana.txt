SCHEME NAME: Kali Bai Bheel Medhavi Chhatra Scooty Yojana
SCHEME NAME (HINDI): काली बाई भील मेधावी छात्रा स्कूटी योजना
SOURCE URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/kbbmcsy
OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://sso.rajasthan.gov.in
CATEGORY: Education / Incentive for Girls
LEVEL: State Government (Rajasthan)
IMPLEMENTING DEPARTMENT: Department of Social Justice and Empowerment / College Education, Government of Rajasthan

OBJECTIVE:
To encourage higher education among Scheduled Tribe (ST) girl students by providing free scooties to meritorious students.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Must be a permanent female resident of Rajasthan
- Must belong to Scheduled Tribe (ST) category
- Must have passed Class 12 in the relevant academic year
- Minimum marks: 65% (RBSE) or 75% (CBSE)
- Must be enrolled as regular student in recognized college/university in Rajasthan for undergraduate course
- No gap of more than one year between Class 12 and graduation admission
- Annual family income must be less than ₹2,50,000
- Students who already received scooty under another state scheme based on previous class marks are NOT eligible for another scooty (but may receive ₹40,000 cash assistance instead)

BENEFITS:
- Free Scooty for meritorious ST girl students (merit-based selection)
- Cash alternative: ₹40,000 lump sum for students who previously received scooty based on Class 10 results from TAD/School Education Department
- Selection based on category-wise merit cut-off list

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Jan Aadhaar Card
- Aadhaar Card
- Class 12 Mark Sheet (showing 65%+ RBSE / 75%+ CBSE marks)
- Caste Certificate (ST)
- Income Certificate (annual family income < ₹2,50,000)
- Domicile Certificate
- College Fee Receipt / Bonafide Certificate
- Bank Account Details

APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. Apply online through Rajasthan SSO Portal at sso.rajasthan.gov.in
2. Login with SSO ID
3. Select "Scholarship (CE)" or "SJMS" portal
4. Fill application and upload documents
5. OR visit e-Mitra center for application assistance
