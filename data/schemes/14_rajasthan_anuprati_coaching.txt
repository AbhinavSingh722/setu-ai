SCHEME NAME: Mukhyamantri Anuprati Coaching Yojana
SCHEME NAME (HINDI): मुख्यमंत्री अनुप्रति कोचिंग योजना
SOURCE URL: https://sje.rajasthan.gov.in
OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://sso.rajasthan.gov.in
CATEGORY: Education / Competitive Exam Coaching
LEVEL: State Government (Rajasthan)
IMPLEMENTING DEPARTMENT: Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Government of Rajasthan

OBJECTIVE:
To provide free, high-quality coaching for competitive examinations to meritorious students from economically weaker sections and socially backward communities in Rajasthan.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Must be a permanent resident of Rajasthan
- Must belong to SC, ST, OBC, MBC (Most Backward Class), EWS, or Minority categories
- Annual family income must be below ₹8,00,000 (₹8 lakh)
- Must have passed required board exams (10th or 12th) as specified for the particular exam
- Must NOT have availed benefits of this scheme previously (one-time benefit)
- Must NOT be a government employee

EXAMS COVERED:
- Civil Services: UPSC (IAS) and RPSC (RAS)
- Professional/Entrance: JEE, NEET, CLAT, CA, CMA
- Government Recruitment: SSC, Banking, Railway, REET

BENEFITS:
- 100% coaching fees paid directly by government to empanelled institutes
- Accommodation Allowance: Up to ₹40,000 per year for food and accommodation for students moving to another city for coaching

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Jan Aadhaar Card (with updated income/caste/bank details)
- Aadhaar Card
- Domicile Certificate
- Caste Certificate (for respective category)
- Income Certificate (annual family income < ₹8 lakh)
- Class 10th / 12th Mark Sheets
- Bank Account Details
- Passport-size photographs

APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. Visit SSO Rajasthan Portal at sso.rajasthan.gov.in
2. Log in with SSO ID
3. Search for Anuprati Coaching Yojana application
4. Fill in required details
5. Upload documents
6. Submit application
7. Check SJE website at sje.rajasthan.gov.in for notification dates and updates

APPLICATION TIMELINE: Notifications typically appear August-September on sje.rajasthan.gov.in
