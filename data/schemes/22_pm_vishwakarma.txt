SCHEME NAME: PM Vishwakarma Yojana
SCHEME NAME (HINDI): प्रधानमंत्री विश्वकर्मा योजना
SOURCE URL: https://pmvishwakarma.gov.in
OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://pmvishwakarma.gov.in
CATEGORY: Skill Development / Artisan Support
LEVEL: Central Government (Central Sector Scheme)
IMPLEMENTING MINISTRY: Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME)

OBJECTIVE:
To provide holistic end-to-end support to artisans and craftspeople (Vishwakarmas) working with their hands and tools through recognition, skill upgradation, modern tools, digital empowerment, and access to credit.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Must be an artisan or craftsperson from one of the 18 identified trades: Carpenter, Boat Maker, Armourer, Blacksmith, Hammer & Tool Kit Maker, Locksmith, Goldsmith, Potter, Sculptor/Stone carver, Cobbler/Shoe Smith, Mason, Basket/Mat/Broom Maker, Doll & Toy Maker, Barber, Garland Maker, Washerman, Tailor, Fishing Net Maker
- Must be 18 years or older
- Must be engaged in the trade in a self-employment capacity
- Must not be working in organized/semi-organized sector or government
- Registration and verification through Gram Panchayat/ULB and CSC
- Family-based scheme: One member per family per trade eligible

BENEFITS:
- PM Vishwakarma Certificate and ID Card (recognition as national artisan)
- 5-7 days Basic Skill Training with Rs 500/day stipend
- 15 days Advanced Skill Training with Rs 500/day stipend
- Modern Tool Kit worth up to Rs 15,000 (e-voucher)
- Collateral-free credit: First tranche up to Rs 1 lakh (at 5% interest), Second tranche up to Rs 2 lakh (at 5% interest)
- Digital empowerment (digital transactions, QR codes, digital skilling)
- Marketing support (National Committee for Marketing, GeM onboarding, trade fairs)
- Total support package worth over Rs 3 lakh per artisan

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Aadhaar Card (mandatory)
- Bank Account details (Aadhaar-linked)
- Mobile Number linked to Aadhaar
- Photographs showing the artisan at work
- Self-declaration of trade/craft

APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. Visit https://pmvishwakarma.gov.in and register
2. Or visit nearest Common Service Center (CSC) for registration
3. Verification by Gram Panchayat/ULB (Urban Local Body)
4. District Implementation Committee approves the application
5. After approval, receive PM Vishwakarma Certificate and ID Card
6. Enroll for skill training at designated training centers
7. Receive tool kit e-voucher after completing training
8. Apply for collateral-free loan at participating banks
9. Helpline: 1800-599-3957 (toll-free)
