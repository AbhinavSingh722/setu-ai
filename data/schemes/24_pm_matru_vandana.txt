SCHEME NAME: Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY)
SCHEME NAME (HINDI): प्रधानमंत्री मातृ वंदना योजना (पीएमएमवीवाई)
SOURCE URL: https://pmmvy.wcd.gov.in
OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://pmmvy.wcd.gov.in
CATEGORY: Maternity Benefit / Women Welfare
LEVEL: Central Government (Centrally Sponsored Scheme)
IMPLEMENTING MINISTRY: Ministry of Women and Child Development

OBJECTIVE:
To provide partial compensation for wage loss so that pregnant women and lactating mothers can take adequate rest before and after delivery, and to improve health-seeking behaviour by providing cash incentives for institutional delivery and child immunization.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Pregnant women and lactating mothers
- For first living child: Total benefit of Rs 5,000 in three installments
- For second child (if girl): Rs 6,000 in one installment
- Women employed in central/state government or PSU or receiving similar benefits under other schemes are NOT eligible
- Must register pregnancy at Anganwadi Centre (AWC) or approved health facility
- Must have MCP (Mother and Child Protection) card

BENEFITS:
- First Child: Rs 5,000 in three installments:
  - 1st installment: Rs 1,000 (on registration of pregnancy at AWC within 150 days of LMP)
  - 2nd installment: Rs 2,000 (after at least one ante-natal check-up after 6 months of pregnancy)
  - 3rd installment: Rs 2,000 (after child birth registration and first cycle of immunization)
- Second Child (Girl): Rs 6,000 in single installment after birth of girl child
- Amount transferred via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to bank/post office account

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Aadhaar Card (of the beneficiary woman)
- Bank/Post Office Account passbook (Aadhaar-linked)
- MCP (Mother and Child Protection) Card
- Aadhaar Card of husband/family member
- Mobile Number

APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. Visit nearest Anganwadi Centre (AWC) or approved health facility
2. Register pregnancy within 150 days of last menstrual period (LMP)
3. Fill Form 1-A for registration and first installment
4. Provide Aadhaar and bank account details
5. After 6 months pregnancy and ANC check-up, fill Form 1-B for 2nd installment
6. After child birth and first immunization, fill Form 1-C for 3rd installment
7. For second girl child, fill Form 2-A after birth
8. All forms available at AWC or online at https://pmmvy.wcd.gov.in
9. Helpline: 181 (Women Helpline) or Anganwadi Worker
