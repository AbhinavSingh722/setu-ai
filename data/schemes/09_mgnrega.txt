SCHEME NAME: Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA)
SCHEME NAME (HINDI): महात्मा गांधी राष्ट्रीय ग्रामीण रोजगार गारंटी अधिनियम (मनरेगा)
SOURCE URL: https://nrega.nic.in
OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://nrega.nic.in
CATEGORY: Employment / Rural Welfare
LEVEL: Central Government
IMPLEMENTING MINISTRY: Ministry of Rural Development

OBJECTIVE:
To enhance livelihood security of rural households by guaranteeing 100 days of wage employment in a financial year to every household whose adult members volunteer to do unskilled manual work.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Must be a citizen of India
- Must be 18 years of age or older
- Must be a resident of a rural area
- Must be willing to perform unskilled manual labor
- Must be part of a household registered with the local Gram Panchayat

BENEFITS:
- 100 days guaranteed wage employment per financial year per rural household
- Unemployment Allowance: If employment not provided within 15 days of application, daily unemployment allowance is payable
- Direct wage payments deposited into worker's Aadhaar-linked bank account
- At least one-third of beneficiaries must be women
- Accident compensation for injury, death, or permanent disability at worksite
- Wages linked to Consumer Price Index for Agricultural Labor (CPI-AL)
- Average daily wage: approximately ₹289-370 (varies by state, revised periodically)

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Application for Job Card (prescribed form or plain paper)
- Proof of residence in rural area
- Aadhaar Card
- Bank/post office account details
- Passport-size photographs

APPLICATION PROCESS:
Offline:
1. Visit local Gram Panchayat office
2. Submit application on prescribed form or plain paper
3. Upon verification, Job Card will be issued

Online:
1. Download UMANG app, register with mobile number
2. Search for "MGNREGA" and select "Apply for Job Card"
3. Physical verification by local authorities is typically required after online submission

After receiving Job Card:
- Apply for work at Gram Panchayat level
- Employment must be provided within 15 days of application
- Work should be provided within 5 km of applicant's residence
