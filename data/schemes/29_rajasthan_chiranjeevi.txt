SCHEME NAME: Rajasthan Chiranjeevi Swasthya Bima Yojana
SCHEME NAME (HINDI): राजस्थान चिरंजीवी स्वास्थ्य बीमा योजना
SOURCE URL: https://chiranjeevi.rajasthan.gov.in
OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://chiranjeevi.rajasthan.gov.in
CATEGORY: Health Insurance
LEVEL: State Government (Rajasthan)
IMPLEMENTING DEPARTMENT: State Health Assurance Agency, Government of Rajasthan

OBJECTIVE:
To provide universal health coverage to all families of Rajasthan, ensuring quality cashless healthcare up to Rs 25 lakh per family per year at empanelled government and private hospitals.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- All families of Rajasthan are eligible
- Free for: NFSA (National Food Security Act) beneficiaries, SECC 2011 families, COVID-19 ex-gratia families, small and marginal farmers, contractual workers, all government health scheme beneficiaries
- Other families: Can register by paying premium of Rs 850/year per family
- Registration through Jan Aadhaar portal mandatory
- No restriction on family size — all members covered

BENEFITS:
- Cashless treatment up to Rs 25 lakh per family per year
- Covers 1,576+ medical packages including surgery, diagnostics, medicines
- 5-day pre-hospitalization and 15-day post-hospitalization expenses covered
- Both government and empanelled private hospitals across Rajasthan
- Coverage for major diseases including heart surgery, cancer treatment, organ transplant, joint replacement, dialysis
- No age limit or exclusion for pre-existing diseases
- Accidental death benefit of Rs 5 lakh
- OPD coverage for select diseases

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Jan Aadhaar Card (mandatory for Rajasthan residents)
- Aadhaar Card (for identity verification)
- Mobile Number linked with Jan Aadhaar
- Premium payment receipt (for non-exempt families)

APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. Visit https://chiranjeevi.rajasthan.gov.in
2. Register using Jan Aadhaar number
3. Verify family details and select members
4. Free category families: Registration is free; no premium
5. Other families: Pay premium of Rs 850/year online via portal
6. Can also register at e-Mitra kiosk or CSC center
7. After registration, receive Chiranjeevi Card digitally
8. For treatment: Visit any empanelled hospital with Jan Aadhaar card
9. Hospital verifies eligibility and provides cashless treatment
10. Helpline: 181 or 0141-2609981
