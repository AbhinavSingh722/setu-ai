SCHEME NAME: Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme (IGNWPS)
SCHEME NAME (HINDI): इंदिरा गांधी राष्ट्रीय विधवा पेंशन योजना
SOURCE URL: https://nsap.nic.in
OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://nsap.nic.in
CATEGORY: Social Security / Pension
LEVEL: Central Government (under National Social Assistance Programme - NSAP)
IMPLEMENTING MINISTRY: Ministry of Rural Development

OBJECTIVE:
To provide monthly financial assistance to widows from Below Poverty Line (BPL) families to meet their basic needs and ensure social security.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Must be a widow (husband deceased)
- Must be between 40 and 79 years of age (beneficiaries aged 80+ covered under separate old-age pension scheme; some states start from age 18)
- Must belong to a family living Below the Poverty Line (BPL) as per Government of India criteria
- Must NOT have remarried
- Must not be already receiving other social welfare pensions (generally)

BENEFITS:
- Central Government pension: ₹300 per month (for ages 40-79)
- State Government top-up: States are advised to contribute at least equal amount
- Total monthly pension varies by state: ₹300 to ₹4,000 depending on state policies
- Payments via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into Aadhaar-linked bank/post office account

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Death certificate of husband
- Age proof (birth certificate, school certificate, etc.)
- BPL Ration Card (proof of BPL status)
- Aadhaar Card
- Bank/post office account details
- Passport-size photographs
- Widow certificate (if applicable in state)

APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. Obtain prescribed application form from local Gram Panchayat, Ward Office, Municipality, or Block Development Office
2. Fill in the form with required details
3. Attach all necessary documents
4. Submit to local authority
5. Local authorities conduct inquiry to verify application
6. Upon verification, beneficiary added to official list
7. Pension credited via DBT

For information: Visit official NSAP portal at nsap.nic.in or local social welfare office
