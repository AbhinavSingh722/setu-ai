SCHEME NAME: PM Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)
SCHEME NAME (HINDI): प्रधानमंत्री किसान सम्मान निधि (पीएम-किसान)
SOURCE URL: https://pmkisan.gov.in
OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://pmkisan.gov.in
CATEGORY: Agriculture / Welfare
LEVEL: Central Government (Central Sector Scheme)
IMPLEMENTING MINISTRY: Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare

OBJECTIVE:
To provide income support to all landholding farmer families across India to supplement their financial needs for procuring various inputs related to agriculture and allied activities.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Must be a landholding farmer family owning cultivable agricultural land
- Land must be in the beneficiary's name
- NOT eligible: institutional landholders; former/present constitutional post holders; former/present Ministers, MPs, MLAs, MLCs, Mayors, District Panchayat Chairpersons; serving/retired government officers (excluding multi-tasking staff/Class IV/Group D); superannuated pensioners with monthly pension ≥ ₹10,000; income taxpayers; professionals (doctors, engineers, lawyers, CAs, architects)

BENEFITS:
- ₹6,000 per year
- Transferred in three equal installments of ₹2,000 every four months
- Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into beneficiary's bank account
- e-KYC mandatory for continued installments

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Aadhaar Card (mandatory for identity verification and e-KYC)
- Bank Account Details (active savings account, Aadhaar-linked preferred for DBT)
- Land Ownership Records (Khata, Khasra, Jamabandi, or Patta proving cultivable land ownership)
- Aadhaar-linked Mobile Number (for OTP verification)

APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. Visit official PM-KISAN website at pmkisan.gov.in
2. Go to "Farmers Corner" → "New Farmer Registration"
3. Enter Aadhaar number, state, and mobile number
4. Fill in personal and land-related details
5. Upload land ownership documents and submit
6. State/UT government verifies details
7. Benefits credited to Aadhaar-seeded bank account upon verification
8. e-KYC can be completed online via PM-KISAN portal, mobile app, or at a Common Service Center (CSC)
