SCHEME NAME: Mukhyamantri Kanya Sumangala Yojana (MKSY)
SCHEME NAME (HINDI): मुख्यमंत्री कन्या सुमंगला योजना (एमकेएसवाई)
SOURCE URL: https://mksy.up.gov.in
OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://mksy.up.gov.in
CATEGORY: Girl Child Welfare / Education
LEVEL: State Government (Uttar Pradesh)
IMPLEMENTING DEPARTMENT: Department of Women and Child Development, Government of Uttar Pradesh

OBJECTIVE:
To provide financial assistance at different stages of a girl child's life — from birth to graduation — to promote positive thinking towards girl children, reduce female foeticide, improve health and educational outcomes, and empower girls.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Must be a resident of Uttar Pradesh
- Annual family income should not exceed Rs 3 lakh
- Maximum two girl children in a family are eligible (3 girls eligible if twins in second delivery)
- Girl child must be born on or after 01 April 2019
- Benefit available from birth to graduation in 6 stages
- Family should not have more than 2 children (excluding twins)

BENEFITS:
- Total benefit of Rs 25,000 (recently enhanced from Rs 15,000) per girl child in 6 installments:
  - Stage 1: Rs 5,000 on the birth of the girl child
  - Stage 2: Rs 2,000 when the girl completes all vaccinations (by age 1)
  - Stage 3: Rs 3,000 when the girl child is admitted to Class 1
  - Stage 4: Rs 3,000 when the girl child is admitted to Class 6
  - Stage 5: Rs 5,000 when the girl child is admitted to Class 9
  - Stage 6: Rs 7,000 when the girl child passes 10th/12th and takes admission in graduate degree or 2-year diploma
- Amount transferred via DBT to mother's/guardian's bank account

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Aadhaar Card (of parents/guardian and girl child if available)
- Birth Certificate of the girl child
- Bank Account details (mother's or guardian's, Aadhaar-linked)
- Family photograph
- Address proof (Voter ID / Ration Card / Utility Bill)
- Income Certificate (annual income not exceeding Rs 3 lakh)
- Affidavit declaring number of children in the family
- School admission proof (for stages 3-6)

APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. Visit https://mksy.up.gov.in and register with mobile number
2. Login and fill the online application form
3. Upload required documents
4. Submit and note the application ID
5. Application verified by District Probation Officer (DPO)
6. After approval, amount credited to bank account for each stage
7. For subsequent stages, apply online when the girl reaches that milestone
8. Offline applications can also be submitted at SDM office or Block Development Office
9. Helpline: 18001800 (toll-free UP Women Helpline)
