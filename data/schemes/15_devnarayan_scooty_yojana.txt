SCHEME NAME: Devnarayan Girls Student Scooty Distribution & Incentive Scheme
SCHEME NAME (HINDI): देवनारायण छात्रा स्कूटी वितरण एवं प्रोत्साहन राशि योजना
SOURCE URL: https://hte.rajasthan.gov.in
OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://sso.rajasthan.gov.in
CATEGORY: Education / Incentive for Girls
LEVEL: State Government (Rajasthan)
IMPLEMENTING DEPARTMENT: Department of Higher Technical Education, Government of Rajasthan

OBJECTIVE:
To encourage higher education among girl students from the Most Backward Classes (MBC) by providing free scooties and financial incentives.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Must be a permanent female resident of Rajasthan
- Must belong to MBC category (specifically: Gurjar/Gujjar, Banjara, Baldiya, Labana, Gadia Lohar, Gadoliya, Raika, Rebari, Gadriya, Gadri, Gayri)
- Must have passed Class 12 with at least 50% marks (from RBSE/CBSE)
- Must be a regular student enrolled in government college or state-funded university in Rajasthan
- No gap year between 12th grade and UG, or between UG and PG
- Annual family income must be less than ₹2,50,000
- Must NOT be receiving benefits from any other government scholarship scheme

BENEFITS:
- Free Scooty: Merit-based distribution (approximately 4,240 per year), includes insurance and one-time petrol supply
- Financial Incentive (for those who don't receive scooty):
  - Undergraduate: ₹10,000 per annum (1st, 2nd, 3rd year)
  - Postgraduate: ₹20,000 per annum (1st year; additional ₹20,000 for 2nd year if 50%+ marks in PG 1st year)

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Aadhaar Card and Jan Aadhaar Card
- Class 12 Mark Sheet (showing 50%+ marks)
- Valid Caste Certificate (MBC/SBC)
- Domicile Certificate
- College Fee Receipt and Bonafide Certificate
- Income Certificate (annual family income < ₹2,50,000)
- Bank Account Details

APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. Apply online via Rajasthan Higher Technical Education (HTE) portal at hte.rajasthan.gov.in
2. OR through Rajasthan SSO portal → Scholarship (College Education) app
3. Fill application form and upload documents
4. Check merit list on HTE portal
