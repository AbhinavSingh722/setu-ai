SCHEME NAME: Bihar Student Credit Card Yojana (BSCCY)
SCHEME NAME (HINDI): बिहार स्टूडेंट क्रेडिट कार्ड योजना (बीएससीसीवाई)
SOURCE URL: https://7nishchay-yuvaupmission.bihar.gov.in
OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://7nishchay-yuvaupmission.bihar.gov.in
CATEGORY: Education Loan / Scholarship
LEVEL: State Government (Bihar)
IMPLEMENTING DEPARTMENT: Education Finance Corporation, Government of Bihar

OBJECTIVE:
To provide educational loans up to Rs 4 lakh at 0% interest to students from Bihar for pursuing higher education, ensuring that no student is denied higher education due to financial constraints.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Must be a resident of Bihar
- Must have passed 12th class (intermediate) from a recognized institution in Bihar
- Student should have been a resident of Bihar at the time of passing 12th
- Age should not exceed 25 years at the time of application (30 years for PG courses)
- The course being pursued must be from a recognized institution (in Bihar or outside)
- Courses covered: BA, BSc, BCom, BCA, BSW, BEd, Engineering, Medical, MBA, MCA, MA, MSc, MCom, and many more professional and general courses
- Both government and private (recognized) institutions eligible

BENEFITS:
- Education loan up to Rs 4 lakh (four lakh rupees)
- 0% interest rate (fully subsidized by the Government of Bihar)
- Covers tuition fee, examination fee, library fee, hostel fee, and living expenses
- Includes cost of laptop, books, and study materials
- No collateral or guarantee required
- Loan repayment starts one year after course completion or six months after getting a job (whichever is earlier)
- For female, transgender, and disabled students: additional 1% interest subsidy on any interest component

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Aadhaar Card (of student and co-applicant/parent)
- 10th and 12th mark sheets and certificates
- College admission letter or enrollment proof
- Income Certificate of parents/guardians
- Bank Account details (of student)
- 2 passport-size photographs
- Domicile/Residence Certificate of Bihar
- PAN Card (of co-applicant)

APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. Visit https://7nishchay-yuvaupmission.bihar.gov.in
2. Register as a new applicant with mobile number and email
3. Fill the online application form with personal and educational details
4. Upload required documents
5. Submit application and note the application ID
6. Visit the nearest DRCC (District Registration cum Counselling Centre) with original documents
7. Counselling and verification done at DRCC
8. After verification, loan sanctioned and disbursed to the institution directly
9. Helpline: 1800-3456-444 (toll-free)
