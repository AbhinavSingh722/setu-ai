SCHEME NAME: National Family Benefit Scheme (NFBS)
SCHEME NAME (HINDI): राष्ट्रीय परिवार लाभ योजना (एनएफबीएस)
SOURCE URL: https://nsap.nic.in
OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://nsap.nic.in
CATEGORY: Welfare / Social Security
LEVEL: Central Government (Centrally Sponsored Scheme)
IMPLEMENTING MINISTRY: Ministry of Rural Development

OBJECTIVE:
To provide a lump-sum financial assistance to the bereaved household on the death of the primary breadwinner, helping the family cope with the immediate financial crisis caused by the loss.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Must be a Below Poverty Line (BPL) family
- The deceased should have been the primary breadwinner of the family
- The deceased should have been aged between 18 and 59 years at the time of death
- Death due to any cause (natural or accidental) is covered
- The surviving members of the household are the beneficiaries
- Claimant should be the eldest surviving member of the household

BENEFITS:
- Lump-sum financial assistance of Rs 20,000 per household
- One-time payment on death of primary breadwinner
- Amount transferred directly to bank/post office account of the claimant
- Payment within 30 days of application approval (target)
- Some states provide additional top-up amounts from state funds

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Death Certificate of the breadwinner
- BPL Card/Certificate or inclusion in SECC-2011 list
- Age proof of the deceased (Aadhaar/Voter ID/Birth Certificate)
- Aadhaar Card of the claimant
- Bank Account details of the claimant
- FIR copy (in case of accidental/unnatural death)
- Relationship proof with the deceased

APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. Contact the local Gram Panchayat (rural) or Municipal Corporation (urban)
2. Obtain the NFBS application form
3. Fill the form and attach all required documents
4. Submit to the Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) or Block Development Officer (BDO)
5. Verification done by Block/District office
6. After approval, amount credited to claimant's bank account via DBT
7. Application must be made within one year of the death
8. Helpline: Contact District Social Welfare Officer or NSAP helpline
