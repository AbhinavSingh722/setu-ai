SCHEME NAME: Rajasthan Uttar Matric Scholarship (Post-Matric Scholarship for SC/ST/OBC)
SCHEME NAME (HINDI): राजस्थान उत्तर मैट्रिक छात्रवृत्ति
SOURCE URL: https://sje.rajasthan.gov.in
OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://sso.rajasthan.gov.in
CATEGORY: Education / Scholarship
LEVEL: State Government (Rajasthan)
IMPLEMENTING DEPARTMENT: Social Justice and Empowerment Department (SJE), Government of Rajasthan

OBJECTIVE:
To support students from disadvantaged communities (SC, ST, OBC, EBC, SBC/MBC, DNT) in pursuing higher education by providing financial assistance through scholarships.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Must be a permanent resident of Rajasthan
- Must belong to SC, ST, OBC, EBC, SBC/MBC, or Denotified Tribes (DNT) category
- Must be enrolled in recognized government or private institution for post-matric courses (ITI, Polytechnic, UG, PG, B.Ed, etc.)
- Annual family income must NOT exceed ₹2,50,000 for SC/ST/SBC students (limits for other categories may vary, often ₹1 lakh to ₹2.5 lakh)
- Must have passed previous qualifying examination
- Must have valid Jan Aadhaar card with updated e-KYC

BENEFITS:
- Fee Reimbursement: 100% reimbursement of compulsory non-refundable tuition fees in government/aided colleges
- Maintenance Allowance: Monthly financial support via DBT (₹250 to ₹1,200/month depending on course level and hosteller/day scholar status)
- Additional Allowances: study tours, book allowances, thesis assistance, special provisions for disabled students (varies by course and scheme)
- Free-Ship Card: SC, OBC, EBC, DNT students can apply to secure admission without upfront fee payment

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Jan Aadhaar Card (mandatory, with updated e-KYC)
- Aadhaar Card
- Caste Certificate (digital, valid for respective category)
- Income Certificate (annual family income within limit)
- Domicile Certificate
- Previous qualification mark sheets
- College enrollment proof
- Bank Account Details (Aadhaar-linked)

APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. Visit SJE Scholarship Portal at sje.rajasthan.gov.in OR Rajasthan SSO Portal at sso.rajasthan.gov.in
2. Log in/Register using Jan Aadhaar ID
3. Select "Scholarship (SJE)" app icon
4. Fill in required details and upload documents
5. Submit application
6. Track application status through SJE portal
