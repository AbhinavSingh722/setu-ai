SCHEME NAME: Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY)
SCHEME NAME (HINDI): प्रधानमंत्री मुद्रा योजना (पीएमएमवाई)
SOURCE URL: https://mudra.org.in
OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://mudra.org.in
CATEGORY: Entrepreneurship / Finance
LEVEL: Central Government (Central Sector Scheme)
IMPLEMENTING MINISTRY: Ministry of Finance (Department of Financial Services)

OBJECTIVE:
To provide micro-loans up to Rs 10 lakh to non-corporate, non-farm small and micro enterprises for income-generating activities, thereby promoting self-employment, entrepreneurship, and financial inclusion.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Any Indian citizen who wants to start or expand a small business
- Non-farm income-generating activities (manufacturing, trading, service sector, allied agriculture)
- No collateral required for loans up to Rs 10 lakh
- Businesses should be non-corporate and non-farm sector
- Existing businesses or new enterprises both eligible
- SC/ST/OBC/Women/Minority entrepreneurs given priority

BENEFITS:
- Shishu: Loans up to Rs 50,000 (for start-ups and new businesses)
- Kishore: Loans from Rs 50,001 to Rs 5,00,000 (for business expansion)
- Tarun: Loans from Rs 5,00,001 to Rs 10,00,000 (for well-established businesses)
- No collateral or guarantee required
- Flexible repayment period (up to 5 years)
- Low interest rates (varies by bank, typically 7.3% to 12%)
- MUDRA Card for working capital needs
- Women entrepreneurs get priority under Mahila Udyami scheme

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Aadhaar Card and PAN Card
- Address Proof (Voter ID / Utility Bill / Passport)
- Business Plan or Project Report (for Kishore/Tarun)
- Proof of business existence (license, registration, etc.) for existing businesses
- Passport-size photographs
- Caste certificate (if applying under SC/ST/OBC priority)
- Bank account statement (last 6 months for existing businesses)

APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. Visit any bank branch (PSU banks, private banks, regional rural banks, MFIs, NBFCs)
2. Or apply online through https://mudra.org.in or https://psbloansin59minutes.com
3. Fill the MUDRA loan application form
4. Submit required documents and business plan
5. Bank evaluates the application and business viability
6. Loan sanctioned and disbursed (Shishu loans processed quickly with minimal documentation)
7. Helpline: 1800-180-1111 (Udyami helpline) or contact MUDRA at 1800-11-0001
