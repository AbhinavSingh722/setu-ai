SCHEME NAME: Central Sector Scheme of Scholarship for College and University Students (CSSS)
SCHEME NAME (HINDI): कॉलेज और विश्वविद्यालय के छात्रों के लिए केंद्रीय क्षेत्र छात्रवृत्ति योजना
SOURCE URL: https://scholarships.gov.in
OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://scholarships.gov.in
CATEGORY: Education / Scholarship
LEVEL: Central Government
IMPLEMENTING MINISTRY: Ministry of Education, Department of Higher Education

OBJECTIVE:
To provide financial assistance to meritorious students from low-income families to meet day-to-day expenses while pursuing higher studies at degree level.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Must have passed Class 12 board examination and secured position above the 80th percentile of successful candidates in respective stream (Science, Commerce, or Humanities)
- Must be pursuing regular undergraduate (UG) or postgraduate (PG) course at recognized college/university
- Annual family income must NOT exceed ₹4,50,000 (₹4.5 lakh)
- Age: Generally between 18 and 25 years
- Students in distance education, correspondence courses, or diploma programs are NOT eligible
- Must not be receiving benefits from any other scholarship scheme

BENEFITS:
- Scholarship amount varies by study level
- Duration: Covers undergraduate and postgraduate studies
- Disbursed via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Class 12 mark sheet (proving above 80th percentile)
- Income Certificate (annual family income ≤ ₹4,50,000)
- Aadhaar Card
- College/university enrollment proof
- Bank account details
- Passport-size photographs

APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. Applications submitted online exclusively through National Scholarship Portal (NSP) at scholarships.gov.in
2. New users must complete One-Time Registration (OTR) on the portal
3. Fill in application form and upload documents
4. Two-level verification: first by educational institution, then by designated State Nodal Agency
5. Application window typically opens July/August and closes October/December — check NSP for exact dates
