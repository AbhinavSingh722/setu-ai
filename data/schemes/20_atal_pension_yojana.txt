SCHEME NAME: Atal Pension Yojana (APY)
SCHEME NAME (HINDI): अटल पेंशन योजना (एपीवाई)
SOURCE URL: https://npscra.nsdl.co.in/scheme-details.php
OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://enps.nsdl.com/eNPS/NationalPensionSystem.html
CATEGORY: Pension / Social Security
LEVEL: Central Government (Central Sector Scheme)
IMPLEMENTING MINISTRY: Ministry of Finance (Department of Financial Services)

OBJECTIVE:
To create a universal social security system for all Indians, especially the unorganized sector workers, by providing a defined minimum pension of Rs 1,000 to Rs 5,000 per month after age 60, based on contributions made during the working years.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Indian citizen aged between 18 and 40 years
- Must have a savings bank account
- Must have Aadhaar and mobile number linked to bank account
- Must not be an income tax payer (rule applicable from Oct 2022 onwards)
- Not a member of any statutory social security scheme (EPFO, ESIC, etc.)
- Workers in the unorganized sector are the primary target group

BENEFITS:
- Guaranteed minimum pension of Rs 1,000, Rs 2,000, Rs 3,000, Rs 4,000, or Rs 5,000 per month after age 60
- Pension amount depends on contribution and age of joining
- Example: Rs 42/month contribution at age 18 gives Rs 1,000/month pension at 60
- Example: Rs 210/month contribution at age 18 gives Rs 5,000/month pension at 60
- Spouse gets same pension amount after subscriber's death
- Nominee gets the accumulated pension corpus after death of both subscriber and spouse
- Government co-contributed 50% of total contribution or Rs 1,000/year (whichever is lower) for subscribers who joined before March 2016

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Aadhaar Card (mandatory)
- Bank Account passbook (savings account)
- Mobile Number linked to bank account

APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. Visit your bank branch (any nationalized or participating bank)
2. Fill the APY registration form (available at the bank)
3. Provide Aadhaar number and mobile number
4. Choose pension amount (Rs 1,000 to Rs 5,000/month)
5. Set up auto-debit from bank account for monthly/quarterly/half-yearly contributions
6. Can also enroll through net banking or mobile banking apps of participating banks
7. Online enrollment at https://enps.nsdl.com/eNPS/NationalPensionSystem.html
8. Helpline: 1800-110-708 (toll-free NPS helpline)
