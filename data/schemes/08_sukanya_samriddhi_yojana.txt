SCHEME NAME: Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)
SCHEME NAME (HINDI): सुकन्या समृद्धि योजना
SOURCE URL: https://www.indiapost.gov.in
OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://www.indiapost.gov.in
CATEGORY: Financial Savings / Girl Child Welfare
LEVEL: Central Government
IMPLEMENTING MINISTRY: Ministry of Finance

OBJECTIVE:
To encourage parents to build a fund for the future education and marriage expenses of their girl child through a government-backed small savings scheme offering high interest rates and tax benefits.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Account can be opened for a girl child below 10 years of age
- The girl child must be an Indian resident
- Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) are NOT eligible
- Maximum 2 accounts per family (one per girl child)
- Third account allowed in case of twins or triplets
- Account opened by parent or legal guardian

BENEFITS:
- Interest Rate: 8.2% per annum (reviewed quarterly by Government of India), compounded annually
- Tax Benefits (EEE - Exempt-Exempt-Exempt):
  - Investment: Tax deduction up to ₹1.5 lakh/year under Section 80C
  - Interest earned: Tax-free
  - Maturity amount: Tax-exempt
- Investment Limits: Minimum ₹250/year, Maximum ₹1.5 lakh/year
- Maturity: 21 years from date of opening
- Deposits required only for first 15 years
- Partial withdrawal (up to 50%) allowed for girl's higher education after Class 10 or age 18
- Premature closure allowed for marriage after age 18

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Birth certificate of the girl child
- Identity proof of parent/legal guardian (Aadhaar, PAN, etc.)
- Address proof of parent/legal guardian
- PAN card of guardian
- Passport-size photographs

APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. Visit any authorized commercial bank branch or India Post office
2. Fill in account opening form
3. Submit required documents
4. Make initial deposit (minimum ₹250)
5. Account passbook will be issued
