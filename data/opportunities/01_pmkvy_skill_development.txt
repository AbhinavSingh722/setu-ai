SCHEME NAME: Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY)
SCHEME NAME (HINDI): प्रधानमंत्री कौशल विकास योजना (पीएमकेवीवाई)
SOURCE URL: https://www.skillindiadigital.gov.in
OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://www.skillindiadigital.gov.in
CATEGORY: Skill Development / Employment
LEVEL: Central Government
IMPLEMENTING MINISTRY: Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship (MSDE)
IMPLEMENTING AGENCY: National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC)

OBJECTIVE:
To provide industry-relevant skill training to Indian youth (including school/college dropouts and unemployed youth) to enhance their employability through market-oriented, demand-driven short-term training, reskilling, and upskilling.

CURRENT PHASE: PMKVY 4.0

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Must be an Indian citizen
- Age: 15-45 years
- Target groups: unemployed youth, school/college dropouts, individuals seeking fresh skilling or upskilling
- For Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL): must have prior informal experience in the job role
- Must have valid Aadhaar card, bank account, and relevant educational certificates

COURSES AVAILABLE:
- 300+ job roles across 40+ sectors including:
  - Agriculture, Automotive, Beauty & Wellness
  - Healthcare, IT & ITeS, Retail, Tourism & Hospitality
  - Emerging fields: AI, Robotics, IoT
  - Construction, Electronics, Plumbing, Apparel
  - And many more sector-specific roles

BENEFITS:
- FREE training (government covers cost)
- NSQF-aligned certification upon successful completion (industry-recognized)
- Placement assistance
- On-the-job training (OJT)
- Induction kits
- Accidental insurance for candidates

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Aadhaar Card
- Bank Account Details
- Educational Certificates (relevant to course)
- Mobile number
- Passport-size photographs

APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. Visit Skill India Digital Hub (SIDH) at skillindiadigital.gov.in
2. Register using mobile number and Aadhaar details
3. Search for available courses by location or interest area
4. Check eligibility for chosen course
5. Apply online and enroll
6. Many centers offer counselling for course selection

RETRIEVAL DATE: 2026-07-12
