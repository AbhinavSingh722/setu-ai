SCHEME NAME: National Career Service (NCS) Portal
SCHEME NAME (HINDI): राष्ट्रीय करियर सेवा (NCS) पोर्टल
SOURCE URL: https://www.ncs.gov.in
OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://www.ncs.gov.in
CATEGORY: Employment / Career Services
LEVEL: Central Government
IMPLEMENTING MINISTRY: Ministry of Labour and Employment

OBJECTIVE:
To act as a one-stop digital platform bridging the gap between job seekers, employers, career counselors, and skill providers. Provides technology-driven job matching, career guidance, and skill development resources.

ELIGIBILITY:
- Registration is free of charge
- Open to all Indian job seekers
- No income or category restrictions

KEY FEATURES FOR JOB SEEKERS:
- Advanced job search and matching (government + private sector)
- Career guidance and counselling
- Psychometric tests for career assessment
- Integration with Skill India Digital Hub (SIDH) for free online training
- Information on internships, apprenticeships, job fairs
- Personalized dashboard for profile, CV upload, NCS Registration Card
- Multilingual support (multiple Indian languages)
- Both public and private sector job listings

SERVICES PROVIDED:
1. Job Matching: Technology-driven matching based on skills, location, and preferences
2. Career Counselling: Expert advice and vocational guidance
3. Skill Development: Free online training and upskilling courses
4. Job Fairs: Information about local and national job fairs
5. Apprenticeship: Connections to apprenticeship opportunities
6. International Employment: Guidance for overseas job opportunities

REGISTRATION PROCESS:
1. Visit www.ncs.gov.in
2. Click "Login/Signup" → select "Register" as "Jobseeker"
3. Provide unique ID (PAN card, Voter ID, Passport, Driving License, or UAN number)
4. Verify mobile number via OTP
5. Complete profile: education, experience, key skills
6. Keep profile updated for better job recommendations

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Any government ID (PAN, Voter ID, Passport, Driving License, or UAN)
- Mobile number
- Email address (recommended)
- Educational certificates (for profile completion)

SPECIAL FEATURES:
- NCS 2.0: Incorporates AI and Machine Learning for smarter job matching
- Support for differently-abled individuals
- Youth-specific guidance and job listings
- No charges or fees at any stage
